About Taste of Africa

Our Mission

At Taste of Africa, our mission is to celebrate and share and promote the rich culinary heritage of Africa with the world. We aim to connect people through food, showcasing the diverse flavors, ingredients, and cooking techniques that define African cuisine.

Brief history of African cuisine and global influence

African cuisine originated with hunter-gatherer societies, progressing to agriculture-based diets in regions like Northeast Africa and spreading to West Africa with the domestication of animals and crops. Culinary traditions have since been enriched by regional trade, foreign encounters, and involuntary exchanges like the transatlantic slave trade, which dispersed African ingredients and techniques globally, influencing dishes in the Americas. Today, African cuisine is celebrated for its diverse ingredients, bold flavors, and significant global impact on dishes like gum, jollof rice, and the use of okra
