Latest Food Trends

African Cuisine on the Rise: Exploring Food Trends and Business Opportunities

December 6, 2024

African cuisine, which is intricately linked to the history of the continent and its diaspora, is essential to cultural identity and culinary influence worldwide. Over the course of centuries of trade and migration, the African diaspora has had a profound impact on global cuisine, popularising dishes like gumbo and jambalaya, which often feature okra, rice, and spices as key ingredients. Most recently, Nigerian chef Hilda Effiong Bassey, professionally known as Hilda Baci, broke the record for the longest cooking marathon (individual) on 13th June 2023.

West African Cuisine Ascends on World Stage

January 31, 2024

Spicy jollof rice? Suya beef kabobs? How about some fufu dough? These are West African dishes, and according to data from Open Table, West African food represented the top trending cuisine of 2023, rising 72% year-over-year. Some experts say the region’s growing momentum on the global culinary scene, however, has been years in the making.

Fusion Cuisine Celebrates African-Asian Connections

September 28, 2025

Innovative chefs are blending African spices with Asian cooking techniques, reflecting historical Indian Ocean trade routes. This trend highlights centuries of cultural exchange, particularly visible in East African coastal cuisine with influences from India and Arabia.
